**Broken / fixed before any result was interpretable:**

1. **Converged on 15% of cells** against 95.0–99.5% for every level/lookback grid
   on disk. Not tuning — identifiability. A channel's baseline power cancels in
   `y2 - y1`, so the differenced outcome retains almost no channel-to-channel
   variance for `(1 | subject:channel)`: `var_channel` 2e-04 against a 3e-02
   residual, where the level grids put it at 1.8e-01 against 3.4e-02. The
   optimizer cannot locate a variance the design set to ~zero, and statsmodels
   reports `converged=False` for the WHOLE fit though the fixed effects are fine.
   Measured on Insula over 10 bins: 3/10 with the term, 10/10 without, 1/24th the
   fit time, `med_between` agreeing to 4e-04 median / 5e-04 max. 15x `maxiter`
   changed nothing (identical to 0e+00) — a flat likelihood, not a truncated
   search. Dropping the random SLOPE instead was strictly worse (0/10), and that
   was the spec the LRT compared against, so `p_lrt_mixture` had been meaningless.
   Fix: `mm.VC_CHANGE` loses `channel`. Convergence went 15% → 75–88%.

2. **A subset arm's control group contained the class it excluded.** `admin`
   arrives pre-filtered to the drug set, so `med_between` is blind to every other
   class: 22.3% of `non_opioid_analgesics` control pairs had an OPIOID between
   the two assessments — the weakest analgesic contrasted against a control group
   holding the strongest. New `--exclude-coexposure` drops those pairs from BOTH
   groups (385 of 1647, 23.4%: 342 controls, 43 doubly-exposed); dropping only
   controls would leave 19.9% opioid co-exposure in the exposed group and let an
   opioid effect be reported as a non-opioid one. `h_since_prior` now spans the
   union of both class sets. NOT applied to the opioid arm, by decision — a weak
   drug in a strong drug's control group biases toward zero, not toward a false
   positive.

   Internal check that the partition is right: the non-opioid arm's control group
   is now byte-identical to the analgesics arm's (1079 pairs, `pain_1` 3.057,
   `d_pain` -0.6849), which is what "no analgesic of any class between" should
   give in both.

- NOMINATION: the broadband-positive medication main effect that the 2-hour
  lookback returned across nearly every region and frequency does NOT reappear
  here — the analgesics arm sits at 53.8% positive, i.e. no broadband tilt. This
  is the comparison the design was built for, and it is the first evidence
  bearing on whether that lookback effect was a multiplicative gain change
  (impedance/reference/amplitude) rather than physiology.
- NOMINATION: `med_between` has OPPOSITE SIGN in the opioid and non-opioid arms
  (-0.0037 vs +0.0088). A non-drug-specific amplitude artifact has no reason to
  flip sign between drug classes, so this argues against the pure-artifact
  reading — but the magnitudes are tiny (< 0.01 log10 power) and the two arms do
  not share an exposed group. Not a finding.
