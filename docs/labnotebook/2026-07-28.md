runs have blank `n_channels`, and treated that as evidence for the converse.
Reverted in `7b5346a`, with a docstring note not to retry it. Lesson for the
`_load_registry` docstring, and generally: a filter that makes a warning count
drop to zero deserves more suspicion than one that makes it rise.
