## 10:25-11:05 — the delta/theta domain-fit failure is OPTIMIZER INSTABILITY, not misspecification

**Ran:** a 2x2 on the diagnosis domain model — {90-day, ever} x {with, without
the parcel-nested random slope}. Slurm 44501830, 44501831, 44509860, 44510830,
~30 min each on `ckeller1`. New flag `--drop-parcel-term` (`5202550`), which
tags its own `noparcel` run directory.

**Why:** delta and theta in the 90-day run carried a non-positive-definite
Hessian after an lbfgs retry, with `var_subj_slope` 300-1100x the four healthy
bands (0.073 / 0.050 against 6.4e-5 to 2.0e-4) and `pain_x_dx` SEs of 0.081 /
0.068 against 0.0035-0.0060. `subj_parcel_slope` is the smallest component in
every band (ratios to residual 1.6e-3 to 4.3e-2 against `BOUNDARY_TOL` 1e-3),
so a flat likelihood direction there was the hypothesis.

**Saw — the hypothesis is WRONG, and the experiment says what is actually going
on.** Dropping the parcel term does not fix the problem; it MOVES it.

| band | 90d WITH parcel | 90d NO parcel |
|---|---|---|
| delta | non-PD, SE 0.081 | **fixed**, SE 0.0071 |
| theta | non-PD, SE 0.068 | **fixed**, SE 0.0055 |
| alpha | fine, SE 0.0060 | **non-PD**, SE 0.0096 |
| gamma | fine, SE 0.0044 | **non-PD**, SE 0.0092 |
| beta / high_gamma | fine | fine |

Four healthy bands either way — a different four. And the label window alone
does the same thing: delta is broken at 90 days and FIXED under `ever` with the
parcel term still in, its `var_subj_slope` falling 0.0735 -> 0.000415 (177x) on
IDENTICAL data. Only the `dx_state` column changed.

So `var_subj_slope` genuinely is ~1e-4. Whenever the optimiser misses that
basin it settles on a spurious 0.05-0.07 with a non-invertible Hessian, and
WHICH band that happens to is arbitrary — any perturbation of the design (the
label window, dropping a variance component) reshuffles it. This is the same
failure already logged for the beta row of the opioid-exclusion run
(2026-09-18), so it is now three independent sightings of one pathology.

**Two things that rule out the obvious alternative readings.** It is NOT
pseudo-replication from dropping the parcel term: over the 20 well-conditioned
cells the betas barely move (Pearson r 0.979 for `pain`, 0.893 for
`pain_x_dx`) and the per-contrast SEs go UP, not down — median ratio 1.085 and
1.110. And it is not real low-frequency heterogeneity, because delta's large
variance evaporates under a relabelling that cannot touch the random effects.

**The no-parcel omnibus is not trustworthy and must not be quoted.** With the
parcel term dropped, `p_omnibus_pain` for beta goes 3.89e-06 -> 8.37e-20 and
`p_omnibus_pain_x_dx` 0.134 -> 9.58e-08, while the marginal SEs rose 8%. The
joint Wald inverts the contrast block's covariance, so it is far more sensitive
to a degraded covariance estimate than any single SE is; in the ever/no-parcel
run alpha reports NOT CONVERGED outright and `pain_x_dx` comes back at 4.46e-20
for beta and 3.21e-10 for gamma. Those are not results.

**Conclusion: KEEP the parcel term. The fix is optimisation, not
specification** — warm-start the three-way from the converged pain-only fit's
parameters (`mm.fit_cell` already takes `start_params`, and the permutation
path already warm-starts for exactly this reason), or fit from several starts
and keep the best likelihood. Until then, exclude any band whose warnings
contain 'not positive definite' rather than reading its interval.

## 11:30 — the narrow beta figure, and why nothing looked significant

**Asked:** the `pain_x_dx` CI for beta looks clear of zero on
`fig_dx_domain.png`, so why is nothing marked significant?

**Answer: the error bars and the significance marks are not the same test, and
that is a figure-design fault of mine.** The bars are UNCORRECTED Wald 95% CIs;
the marks are BH-corrected over the whole 30-cell `pain_x_dx` family. A CI clear
of zero is just uncorrected p < 0.05. On the noparcel/ever run exactly two cells
clear zero and neither is rejected:

| circuit | band | beta | p | p_bh (30 cells) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cognitive | beta | +0.0115 | 0.0034 | 0.103 |
| Modulatory | beta | +0.0101 | 0.0436 | 0.654 |

Both figures now say this in the caption. Not a bug — but it was invisible.

**Ran:** new `plot_dx_band_difference.py`. One band, per circuit, MDD+ vs MDD-,
no omnibus. `--band` has NO DEFAULT on purpose, because the band choice IS the
multiple-comparison family and on this cohort the family decides the answer.

**Saw — the one real candidate in this whole analysis.** WITH the parcel term,
`ever` label, beta band:

| circuit | MDD+ - MDD- | 95% CI | p | p_bh (5 circuits in beta) | p_bh (30 cells) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cognitive | **+0.0108** | +0.0030 to +0.0186 | 0.0069 | **0.034** | 0.205 |
| Modulatory | +0.0100 | -0.0042 to +0.0241 | 0.168 | 0.419 | 0.754 |
| Control | +0.0029 | -0.0074 to +0.0133 | 0.579 | 0.885 | 0.974 |
| Affective | +0.0013 | -0.0086 to +0.0111 | 0.803 | 0.885 | 0.974 |
| Sensory | +0.0007 | -0.0082 to +0.0095 | 0.885 | 0.885 | 0.974 |

So Cognitive/beta is significant at q=0.05 **if and only if** the family is the
5 circuits within beta. Over all 30 circuit x band cells it is p_bh = 0.205.
Both numbers are arithmetically right; which is honest depends on whether beta
was pre-specified, which is not a fact in the data. Both columns are written to
`dx_beta_difference.csv` so the choice stays visible.

Worth noting in its favour and not over-weighting: the same prefrontal/beta
cluster was nominated independently by the REGION-level band model (dlPFC,

data, so it is a consistent nomination and not independent confirmation.

**Ran:** new `plot_dx_contrast_bands.py`, on all four diagnosis domain runs.

Three design points worth recording because each changes a number:

1. **Control is dropped from the REPORT, not the model.** It is the treatment
   reference and the scheme's negative-control network, so its own contrast was
   the reference's marginal value. No coefficient changes. What changes is the
   family: 5 circuits -> 4.
2. **The family is RE-CORRECTED, not filtered.** Inheriting the pipeline's
   30-cell `p_bh` and then hiding rows would leave the survivors corrected for
   tests no longer reported. BH is recomputed over exactly the reported cells
   and the pipeline value is kept beside it as `p_bh_pipeline_grid`.
3. **Ill-conditioned bands leave the figure AND the family.** Which bands those
   are is a property of the SPECIFICATION, not the frequency.

**Saw — the beta result does not survive an honest correction.** Cognitive/beta
is the top cell in every specification and its sign and magnitude are stable,
but nothing is BH-significant anywhere once the family is the reported grid:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ever, WITH parcel | 20 cells (4x5, theta out) | +0.01076 | 0.0069 | **0.137** | no |
| 90d, NO parcel | 16 cells (4x4, alpha+gamma out) | +0.00935 | 0.0227 | 0.363 | no |
| 90d, WITH parcel | 16 cells (4x4, delta+theta out) | — | — | — | no |
| ever, NO parcel | 16 cells | — | — | — | no |

So the p_bh = 0.034 from `fig_dx_beta_difference.png` was a 5-test family --

## 17:00 — Domain-level consistency: sign agreement and tau/I2

**Ran:** the across-subject consistency analysis at DOMAIN level, as the
counterpart of the region-level map logged at 16:09. Per-subject slopes are
unpooled OLS lines per (subject, domain, band), recomputed from the run's own
view in seconds rather than saved by the fit -- which also means the script
runs against any existing domain run, parcel-level ones included.
`analysis/plot_domain_consistency.py`, `sbatch/domain_consistency.sbatch`.

**Output:** `<the 15:31 ROI-level domain run>/consistency/`
**Commit:** `517c5db`
**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**On the variance measure:** Sabra asked for "% of subjects with the same slope
or the std or some other measure of variance". The plain SD of the subject
slopes is computed and written to `domain_consistency_cells.csv` but is
deliberately NOT given a figure. Per-subject precision here varies by close to
an order of magnitude, so the raw spread is mostly a map of how noisily each
patient was measured. **tau** (DerSimonian-Laird) is that spread with the
expected sampling contribution subtracted, in the units of beta; **I2** is the
share that survives. Both are UPPER BOUNDS -- Q is computed against OLS SEs
that assume independent epochs and pain reports are autocorrelated.

**Saw:**

- **tau/|beta| separates the significant cells cleanly.** In the 12
  BH-significant cells tau/|beta| runs 0.8-1.4; in the null cells it runs 2-54.
  So where there is an effect, the between-patient spread is about the SIZE of
  the effect -- not smaller. A real group effect here coexists with patients
  differing from each other by as much as the average effect.
- I2 is 48-76% nearly everywhere, i.e. most of the spread survives the noise
  subtraction. Read as an upper bound, but the flatness across cells is itself
  the point: no domain is notably more homogeneous than another.
- Sign agreement, excess over the cell's own null: Modulatory theta/alpha/beta/
  gamma +27/+28/+23/+23, Cognitive beta +26, Affective delta +25, Cognitive
  delta +20, Sensory delta +18, Sensory beta +14.
- **Modulatory's high agreement is n=20, not n=46.** Its null is 56-58% against
  Sensory's 53-55%, because the null RISES as n falls. 85% at n=20 and 80% at
  n=46 are not the same evidence. This is the trap the raw percentage invites
  and the reason the excess panel exists.
- Affective/gamma comes out at 43% against a 53% null. That is not a reversed
  effect; its group beta is +0.0001, i.e. the sign being agreed with is
  arbitrary.

- notable: `consistency/fig_domain_consistency_heterogeneity.png` — tau and I2,
  with tau/|beta| annotated per cell.
- notable: `consistency/fig_domain_consistency_strip.png` — every patient as a
