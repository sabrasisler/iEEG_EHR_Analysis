## 13:25 — Opioid administration as a factor in the band-power models

**Ran:** the band-power models again with `--med-model both --drug-set opioids`:
an epoch counts as medicated when any opioid administration falls in the 2 h
before its pain score. Three fits per cell — pain-only, the decomposed model
(med split within/between plus its interaction with pain), and the matched model
(pain as an 11-level FACTOR, so no linearity assumption). 19 regions x 6 bands,
Lateral Temporal still excluded. `fit_decomposed` and `fit_matched` are reused
from `run_mixed_model_med_strata`, not reimplemented.

**Output:** `$DERIV/analysis/pain/bandpower/mixed_effects/paperbands6hg200-roiv2ofc-opioids/paperbands6_mixedlm_20260917-130515/`
**Commit:** `f2c21d7` (+dirty from unrelated files)
**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**Saw:**

- Join: 836/2406 epochs (34.7%) opioid-medicated; 49 of 51 subjects have any
  opioid administration, sub-189 and sub-202 have none so all their epochs read
  as unmedicated. Confounding by indication reproduced exactly as `med_state`
  documents it: **NRS 4.67 medicated vs 2.22 unmedicated**.
- Four BH families, corrected separately: pain 30/114, **med_within 45/114**,
  pain x med interaction 19/114, matched 0/114.
- **The within-patient opioid association is LARGER than the pain association it
  was supposed to be confounding.** |medw| median 0.0199 against pain betas of
  0.01-0.02, and the biggest medw cells are ones where pain does nothing:
  Occipital delta medw +0.075 (pain -0.001, p=0.86), S1 delta +0.068 (pain
  p=0.70), dlPFC theta +0.048 (pain p=0.87). Mostly POSITIVE and
  low-frequency-dominant, which is what sedation would look like.
- **The matched model's zero is POWER, not absence.** Effect magnitudes agree —
  |matched| median 0.0136 and |nonparam| 0.0136 against |medw| 0.0199 — with 93%
  sign agreement, r=+0.74 (medw vs matched) and r=+0.67 (medw vs the model-free
  per-subject difference). What changes is precision: the matched SE runs 0.0172
  against medw's 0.0053, **3.2x larger**, because conditioning on an 11-level NRS
  factor spends that much of the design. So three estimators with quite different
  assumptions agree on sign and size, and only the cheapest one clears BH.
- **19 cells where dosing significantly CHANGES the pain slope**, mostly positive
  interactions against negative or near-zero baseline slopes (OFC delta: pain
  -0.0109, interaction +0.0067; also Thalamus gamma, Insula high_gamma, PCC
  beta/gamma). Where that holds, pain and opioid are not additively separable and
  the pain-only beta is not the pain effect.
- notable: `fig_med_effects.png` — five panels, own colour scale each, BH within
  each term's own family.

**Broken / needs rerun:** nothing, but Occipital rests on 10 subjects (9 matched)
so its +0.075 is the thinnest number in the table.
