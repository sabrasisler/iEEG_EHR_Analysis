**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**Saw:**

- 114 cells, 113 converged, **30 BH-significant**. The delta-down / beta-up
  pattern is unchanged from the 70-170 run.
- **Extending high_gamma worked as intended.** Thalamus high_gamma went from
  z=+2.52, p_bh=0.048 (70-170 Hz, 191 bins) to **z=+3.28, p_bh=0.0071** (70-200
  Hz, 242 bins). The band now covers the ~124-250 Hz structure the
  native-resolution map showed, instead of truncating it at 170.
- notable: `fig_check_residuals.png` — three results at once. Residuals vs fitted

- **The residual ACF is strongly positive: 0.42 at lag 1 (median 2.0 h), 0.33,

  (p=1.9e-07, 34/40 subjects share the sign) vs between +0.0486 (p=0.44). Same
  sign, so no Simpson's paradox here; the between-subject relation is ~3x larger
  and far too uncertain at 40 points to say anything.

  low-variance mass, so the within-subject contrast has real spread to work with.

assertion that raises if statsmodels' semantics change; the figure above is the
