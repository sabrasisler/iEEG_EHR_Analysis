**Saw:**

- The pattern is the same one. Matching each old log bin to the mean of the
  native bins inside it, old vs new beta correlate r = 0.978 (Spearman 0.969,
  OLS slope 1.04, sign agreement 92.6% over 798 matched cells). Per band r runs
  0.94-0.98 below 60 Hz and drops to 0.897 / 86% sign agreement above it — which
  is where the old bins were widest and the notch treatment differs most.
- notable: `fullres_grid_mixedlm_20260916-112734/fig_fullres_spectra.png` — the
  low-frequency decrease to beta-band increase profile is continuous and smooth
  in every region, and S1/M1 show a double peak near ~10 and ~18 Hz that the
  44-bin axis could not have resolved.
- 9,332 of 9,723 cells converged (96%). Non-convergence concentrates at high
  frequency (4.7% above 120 Hz vs 1.6% at 1-4 Hz) and in the largest regions
  (Lateral Temporal 87, Insula 65).
- Cluster test, RAW arm: 27 clusters, NONE significant. Not a power failure at
  the within-region stage — the smallest within-region p is 0.0061 — but the
  two-stage correction needs the minimum p below 0.05/21 = 0.0024 for BH to
  reject anything, and the broadband low-frequency offset makes the observed
  runs (and the null's) enormous, so p bottoms out just short.
- Cluster test, DETREND arm: 52 clusters, 17 significant under the sign-flip
  null and 25 under the predictor-shuffle null. Coherent spatial split in the
  nominations: beta-band increases in sensorimotor and lateral/medial frontal
  (M1, S1, S2/PO, dlPFC, IFG/vlPFC, dmPFC/SMA, Occipital), high-frequency
  DECREASES in M1/S1/Parietal, high-frequency INCREASES in Thalamus and
  Hippocampus.
- notable: `fullres_cluster_20260916-135008/fig_fullres_clusters.png` — raw arm
  has no outlines at all, detrended arm does; that contrast is the figure's
  point.
- The +/-2 Hz notch takes 36 of 499 bins, not 8. The comparison is inclusive at
  both ends, so it is 9 bins per harmonic; the comments in `psd_params.py` and
  `fullres_reader.notch_freqs` said 8 and were corrected in the same commit.
- The epoch-mean view covers 81 subjects, 30 of them NOT in the locked discovery
  cohort. Materializing a view over everything on disk is fine, but the analysis
  path now gates the split before eligibility is computed.
