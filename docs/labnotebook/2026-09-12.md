**Saw:**

- **The hypothesis is dead, and cleanly.** Paired over all 45 subjects, random CV:
  bipolar mean r = +0.1142, Laplacian +0.1101. Paired difference **-0.004**,
  Wilcoxon **p = 0.59**. Laplacian better in 22/45 — a coin flip. Blocked CV is
  the same story (-0.0029 vs -0.0208, p = 0.58).
- **Per-subject agreement is Spearman rho = +0.925** (random CV). Two pipelines
  sharing only the raw voltage and the band definitions rank subjects almost
  identically. That is a stronger statement than the null itself: the decodable
  signal is robust to the spatial filter.
- Every earlier conclusion REPLICATES on this independent pipeline — the
  group-level effect (20/45 subjects beat their own null where ~2 are expected),
  the roughly-halving under blocked CV, and the negative null location
  (r ~ -0.20 in both). The blocked-CV attenuation is therefore not an artifact of
  the Welch estimator.
- Classification: mean AUC 0.593 +/- 0.109 over 35 subjects, 10/35 at p<0.05,

  subjects, only **7 (28%)** fail the paper's inclusion criteria; the other 18
  meet every one and still do not decode. Fisher exact **p = 0.473**. Restricting
  to the 35 eligible subjects moves the group mean 0.110 -> 0.122 — real, but
  nowhere near the gap to the paper's numbers. All 10 failures are median-pain-0
  subjects, and three of THOSE decode well (252 r=0.372, 227 r=0.359,
  244 r=0.242, all p=0.01) — consistent with 0-vs-nonzero being an easier
  discrimination than low-vs-high.
