Three things the figure cannot show and says so in its caption: a parcel is
painted UNIFORMLY (one beta per region, not a per-vertex map, so the boundaries
are the atlas's); colour does not encode coverage, so an 11-subject region looks
exactly as solid as a 55-subject one; and hemispheres are pooled in the model, so
left and right are identical BY CONSTRUCTION rather than by a measured symmetry.

Two implementation notes worth not rediscovering. nilearn's `plot_surf_contours`
raises `Vertices in parcellation do not form region` on a level with no vertices,
which is what a significant SUBCORTICAL region is on a cortical surface — levels
have to be filtered to regions that exist on that surface. And a 3D axes cannot
be packed by any of matplotlib's layout engines, so each panel is rendered alone
onto a transparent canvas, cropped to its own ink, and composed as an image in a
2D grid; that is the only reason the figure is not two-thirds white space.
