**Ran:** New level-2 question `pain_coupling`. Each administration of the four
most-administered analgesics joined to the pain score charted in the 30 min
before it (`merge_asof`, backward, `by=[subject, session]`), then binned:

**Output:** `$DERIV/analysis/meds/pain_coupling/pain_score_bars/pain_score_bars_20260903-165936/`
**Commit:** `7849145f87bf` +dirty <!-- dirty = the same unrelated in-flight pain_coef/dtype_audit files as the earlier runs; the med_analysis code is exactly at this commit -->
**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**Saw:**

- **A clean potency ladder in the median preceding score:** acetaminophen 4
  (IQR 3-6), hydrocodone-acetaminophen 6 (5-7), oxycodone 7 (5-8),
  hydromorphone 8 (7-9). Share of administrations given at a score of 7+ runs
  23% / 50% / 53% / 77% across the same four. Nobody chose this ordering — it
  falls out of the join, and it is the first thing in this dataset that looks
  like dosing tracking reported pain rather than the clock.
- Acetaminophen is the only one of the four with meaningful low-score use: 5.7%
  of its administrations follow a score of 0, versus 0.5-1.0% for the three
  opioids. It peaks at score 3; the opioids peak at 7 (hydrocodone-
  acetaminophen, oxycodone) and 8 (hydromorphone).
- Score 1 is nearly empty across all four drugs (8 administrations total, all
  acetaminophen) — the scale is used coarsely in practice, which is why
  `counts_by_score` reindexes the full 0-10 range rather than letting the gap
  silently close.
- 1,113 of 1,509 administrations linked (74%); 396 dropped for having no
  assessment in the 30 min before the dose. Median gap is 0.0 min for all four
  drugs, i.e. the modal case is a score and a dose charted in the same minute.
- **Reads against Fig 3.** Fig 3's normalized-dose row sat near 1.0 flat across
  hospital days while administration RATE tapered. Fig 5 says the same thing
  from the other side: which drug gets reached for tracks the score, and the
  taper is in frequency. Consistent, not redundant.

**Broken / needs care:** Fig 5's totals do NOT reconcile with Fig 1's, by
design — the 30-min window is an inclusion criterion, so 26% of administrations
are absent. The excluded count is printed on the figure for that reason. And
the 45% same-minute tie rate is the load-bearing assumption of the whole panel:
it is defensible from charting workflow and the shape is unchanged without it,
but the data cannot prove ordering within a minute (DECISIONS 2026-09-03, call 2).
