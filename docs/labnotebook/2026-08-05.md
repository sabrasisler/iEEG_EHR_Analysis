## 09:00 — Cluster permutation test, and what its control actually measured

`analysis/cluster_permutation.py` + `--cluster-test`. Method in the new
`docs/cluster_permutation.md`. **Commit:** `5e3ce07`, 265 tests.

**The negative control failed, and that was the most useful result of the day.**
Testing the 0-pain bin against zero gave significant clusters. Diagnosed rather than
patched:

```
none: max |group mean| 0.0179 z, median 0.0037
high: max 0.66,            median 0.067        -- 18x to 33x larger
corr(none deviation, n_channel_epochs_dropped_coverage) = +0.651
corr(none deviation, mask_excluded_frac)                = +0.425
```

The baseline pools 0-pain WINDOWS; a reported value averages EPOCHS. QC masking
leaves epochs with unequal surviving-window counts, so the two differ and the bin
cannot be exactly zero. Two epochs of 300 and 30 windows satisfy
`300·m_A + 30·m_B = 0`, so the equal-weighted mean is `0.45·m_B` — the depleted
epoch takes 50% of the weight for 9% of the windows.

Conclusion: equal-weighting epochs is the same principle `CLAUDE.md` applies to
subjects, so the offset is its price and the CONTROL was badly conceived, not the
pipeline. `none` is also circular — those are the very windows that define the
baseline. It is now reported as a measured NOISE FLOOR, and every cluster carries
`mean_abs_z` and `floor_ratio` beside its p-value, because a t-test will happily
call 0.004 z significant. Queued in `TASKS.md`: a split-half baseline (the real
non-circular control) and an epoch-retention QC threshold, which would shrink the
weighting inequality at source. Open questions in `SCRATCHPAD.md`.

## 09:30 — `roi_v2`: 21 regions, and the bug that would have hidden it

New scheme in `config/roi_schemes.py`, additive — `default` untouched, so existing
runs stay reproducible. Splits ACC → rACC/dACC, OFC → mOFC/lOFC, and the
`Frontal (other)`/`Temporal` catch-alls into M1, dmPFC/SMA, IFG/vlPFC, MTL (other),
Lateral Temporal, Auditory, Parietal (other). Occipital promoted to a real ROI as a
quasi-control. Frontopolar (2 subjects) and Cerebellum (0) dropped on a measured
tally of the `channel_meta` tables. **Commit:** `7c21cd7`, 280 tests.

**`config.ROI_REGIONS` is the default 15 regions, and three places in the plotting
path filtered against it.** Under `roi_v2` every region whose NAME is absent from
the old list — 13 of 21 — would have been silently dropped, leaving 8 rows in a
figure that looked entirely normal. The region order now comes from the view's own
sidecar via `view_tables.roi_regions_for`. Documented in `view_registry.md` AXIS 6.

**Output:** `$DERIV/analysis/pain/psd_physiology/region_freq_heatmap/{zscore,delta}-relpain-roiv2/`
and `region_spectrum/{zscore,delta,raw}-relpain-roiv2/`
**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**I predicted fewer clusters would survive at 21 regions, because the BH family
grows from 15 to 21. The opposite happened:**

| | 15 regions | 21 regions |
|---|---|---|
| `high` significant | 11 | 17 |
| `high − low` significant | 2 | 6 |
| `low` | 0 | 0 |
| `none` above 3× floor | 0 | 0 |

Splitting the catch-alls GAINED sensitivity: `Frontal (other)` had been averaging
dmPFC/SMA, IFG/vlPFC and M1 together, and those three now show distinct
individually-significant beta/low-gamma clusters. Diluting a real effect across a
heterogeneous region cost more power than the stricter correction does.

Nominations from the 21-region map, all EXPLORATORY:
- The sensorimotor strip (M1, S1, S2/PO) is the clearest block, ~13–43 Hz increase.
  M1 was previously buried inside `Frontal (other)`.
- rACC shows a strong low-frequency decrease; dACC shows essentially nothing. Merged
  as "ACC" they partly cancelled. Same story for lOFC (decrease) vs mOFC (nothing).
- Occipital, the quasi-control, has NO significant cluster in either panel despite a
  visible warm patch. Reassuring — argues against a global/artifactual driver.
- `low` produced zero significant clusters in either view; `high − low` isolates
  S1 and S2/PO. That contrast cancels the broadband offset, so it is the most
  interpretable of the three.

Noise floor 0.0181 z (zscore) / 0.0132 (delta), essentially unchanged from the
15-region run.

## 09:18 — Electrode coverage glass brain, and sub-071

`analysis/plot_electrode_locations.py`. **Commit:** `3bfa9b4`

The coordinates were already in the NWB and simply were not being extracted:
`MNI_coord_1/2/3` in the bipolar electrodes table is the MIDPOINT of the pair's two
contacts — verified against sub-019, where LA1 and LA2 average to exactly the stored
pair value. That is the virtual electrode position, and what `roi_schemes.py` names
as the eventual right basis for region assignment. `channel_meta` now carries it
plus `wm_vs_gm_anode` and `seeg_ecog_anode`, with a `SCHEMA_VERSION` and a column
check so a table written before the columns existed rebuilds rather than being
returned silently incomplete. All 60 rebuilt, 0 failed.

**Output:** `$DERIV/analysis/pain/psd_physiology/electrode_locations/roiv2/discovery_20260805-091838/`
4631 pairs, 55 subjects. 3247 contacts outside the ROI scheme, 15 out of MNI bounds.

**The figure has 55 subjects where the analysis has 56, and running that down found
a data problem.** sub-071's MNI coordinates are physically impossible: x −382..371,
y −784..836, z −216..89, against an MNI152 extent of roughly ±90 / −130..90 /
−80..110. Off by a factor of 4–9 — a units or registration failure, not noise. It is
the only affected subject; the entire out-of-bounds count is sub-071's ROI-mapped
remainder.

The figure excluded it correctly. The ANALYSIS may not: sub-071 contributes region
rows to every `roi_v2` heatmap and spectrum, and its DK labels come from the same
localization. Queued in `TASKS.md` as a decision plus the upstream fix — nothing in
the QC tree currently looks at coordinates at all, and a bounds check would have
caught this before it entered any analysis. sub-071 already had two all-`-inf` PSD
runs on the scratchpad; two unrelated defects in one subject is worth looking at as
a whole.
