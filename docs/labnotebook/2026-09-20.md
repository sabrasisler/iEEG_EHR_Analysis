## 17:07 — do MDD patients report more pain? (stratum description, no model)

**Ran:** new `src/ieeg_ehr/analysis/plot_dx_pain.py`, on `-p dev`, seconds. No
view, no QC mask, no model — it reads the de-identified `pain-scores.csv` and
`diagnoses.csv` tables directly. This is the check that has to come before any
map split by diagnosis: if cases simply report pain differently, a slope
difference can come from the predictor's distribution rather than from encoding.

**Output:** `$DERIV/analysis/pain/mdd/stratum_description/dx_pain_levels_20260920-170746/`
**Commit:** `d26578e` (pushed) — the run itself predates the commit by minutes and
logged `+dirty`; the code is unchanged between the two.
**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**Saw:** the strata are balanced on the pain variable, which is the good case.
17 cases / 34 controls under the 90-day rule.

| metric | MDD | control | Welch p | Hedges g |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean NRS | 2.84 | 2.90 | 0.894 | -0.03 |
| within-subject NRS SD | 2.35 | 2.20 | 0.288 | +0.28 |
| n charted ratings | 58.3 | 47.5 | 0.098 | +0.54 |
| fraction of ratings at 0 | 0.34 | 0.37 | 0.653 | -0.13 |

The second row is the one that bears on the slope contrast — the slope is
estimated over the range a patient spans, so a spread difference is a precision
difference that can masquerade as an effect. It is small and not significant.
The largest effect is that MDD patients are CHARTED more often (g = +0.54,
p = 0.098), which is a nursing-attention difference, not a pain difference, and
it acts on the slope through n rather than through bias.

## 17:36 — MDD as a moderator of the band x region pain slope

**Ran:** `run_bandpower_mixed --dx-model interaction --dx mdd
--dx-window-days 90 --question mdd`, Slurm 44409072, ~14 min on `ckeller1`.
One pooled fit per cell:

    log10_power ~ NRS_within * dx_state + NRS_submean
                  + (NRS_within || subject) + (1 | subject:channel)

The random effects are the pain-only model's, unchanged. `dx_state` is a
subject-level 0/1 constant so it gets no within/between split — there is no
within-subject part to split — and the by-subject random intercept is what its
standard error is judged against.

An earlier submission (44408714) died instantly on `args[@]: unbound variable`:
a pre-existing bug in `sbatch/bandpower_mixed.sbatch` where an EMPTY bash array
expands to an unbound variable under `set -u`. It had survived every prior run
because those all passed `EXCLUDE_REGIONS`. Fixed in `9897aa4`'s parent.

**Output:** `$DERIV/analysis/pain/mdd/mixed_effects/paperbands6hg200-roiv2ofc/paperbands6_mixedlm_20260920-172137/`
**Commit:** `d26578e` (pushed; tree dirty from unrelated in-progress files)
**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**Saw:** 90 of 120 cells fitted, 30 refused because a stratum held under 8
subjects — exactly the five regions predicted from coverage beforehand (dACC 4,
Occipital 5, rACC 5, Auditory 6, M1 7 MDD subjects). **Zero BH-significant
interactions; the smallest `dx_ix_p_bh` is 0.573.**

The reason is precision, not absence of effect, and the run measures it:

- median |interaction beta| = 0.00499, against median |pain beta| = 0.00417 —
  the candidate differences are the same SIZE as the main effects;
- median interaction SE = 0.00643, against median pain SE = 0.00330 — the
  interaction is **2x noisier**, which is the whole cost of a between-subject
  17-vs-32 contrast.

So this reads as NOT RESOLVED AT THIS N, not as "no difference". Strongest
uncorrected cells, all NOMINATIONS and none surviving correction:

| region | band | control | MDD | difference | p (uncorr) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dmPFC/SMA | alpha | -0.01127 | +0.00568 | +0.01694 | 0.013 |
| dlPFC | beta | +0.01014 | +0.02769 | +0.01755 | 0.014 |
| OFC | beta | +0.00041 | +0.01071 | +0.01030 | 0.026 |
| Insula | alpha | -0.00245 | +0.00647 | +0.00892 | 0.034 |
| S1 | high_gamma | +0.00656 | -0.00592 | -0.01248 | 0.042 |

54 of 90 cells have a positive difference and 36 negative, so there is no
global sign asymmetry; the nominated cells are frontal and in the direction of
a STRONGER positive slope in MDD.

- notable: `fig_dx_effects.png` — three panels. The two stratum panels are
  deliberately NOT outlined for significance; only the difference panel is.
  Outlining each arm separately is the difference-of-significance fallacy, and
  with 16 vs 32 subjects the MDD arm has wider SEs everywhere from power alone.

**Ran:** `run_domain_model --dx-model interaction --dx mdd --dx-window-days 90
--question mdd`, Slurm 44408717, 33 min. Full three-way per band:

    log10_power ~ NRS_within * C(domain) * dx_state + NRS_submean

so `p_omnibus_pain_x_dx` — the joint Wald over the three-way block — is the
literal test of "do different circuits change differently in depression".
Refused alongside `--med-model`, which would be a four-way.

**Commit:** `d26578e`; figure regenerated at `9897aa4`
**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**Saw:** no omnibus is significant. delta 0.117, theta 0.995, alpha 0.767,
beta 0.134, gamma 0.133, high_gamma 0.414. BH families: `pain_x_dx` 0/30
rejected, `dx` 0/30, while `pain` itself still rejects 6/30 — the pain effect
is there, the diagnosis modulation of it is not resolved.

The one coherent nomination agrees with the band map: in **beta**, the

## 18:10 — the antidepressant confound is real and measurable

**Ran:** ad-hoc on `-p dev`, straight from the raw `med-admin.csv` files. The
existing derived admin table (`admin_table_20260903-145332`) is **analgesics
only** — level1 has one value, `Analgesics` — so the antidepressant question
cannot be asked of it and the raw MAR had to be re-scanned with
`med_taxonomy`'s Antidepressants names, `mar_action == 'Given'`.

**Saw:** MDD-labelled patients are far more likely to be receiving an
antidepressant during the EMU admission.

|  | no antidepressant | on antidepressant |
|---|---|---|
| control | 33 | 1 |
| MDD | 12 | 5 |

Fisher exact OR = 13.75, p = 0.012. 29% of cases vs 3% of controls. Drugs seen:
bupropion, citalopram, duloxetine, escitalopram, mirtazapine, sertraline,
venlafaxine.

This is the competing explanation for any MDD slope difference, since SSRIs and
SNRIs alter cortical oscillatory power directly, and it is now quantified rather
than asserted. The mitigating fact is that it is only 5 of 17 cases — the MDD
arm is mostly untreated IN HOSPITAL, so dropping those 5 leaves 12 cases and is
a runnable sensitivity analysis rather than an empty one. Nothing in today's
runs adjusts for it.

## 18:06 — `ever` window as the pre-specified sensitivity analysis

**Ran:** `run_bandpower_mixed --dx-model interaction --dx-window-days 0`
(0 means EVER: any code dated at or before `session_start`), Slurm 44416025.

**Output:** `$DERIV/analysis/pain/mdd/mixed_effects/paperbands6hg200-roiv2ofc/mdd_ever_mixedlm_20260920-180631/`
**Commit:** `9897aa4` (pushed)
**Index:** logged in `docs/analyses_run.md`

**Saw:** the `ever` rule labels **26 of 51** cases against the 90-day rule's 17,
and only **6** of those are billing-code-only against the 90-day rule's 11. So
`ever` is both a better-balanced split (26 vs 25) and a better-supported label,
at the cost of including remote history rather than current episode.

96 of 120 cells fitted (24 refused, against 30), and **still zero
BH-significant** — min `dx_ix_p_bh` = 0.444 against 0.573.

**The two windows agree, which is the useful part.** Over the 90 cells both
fitted: sign agreement 73/90, and Pearson r = **0.866** between the two sets of
interaction betas. The conclusion does not hinge on the labelling choice.

The `ever` nomination list is also more coherent than the 90-day one — the top
three are all prefrontal and all beta, and in each the MDD slope is about 3x the
control slope:

| region | band | control | MDD | difference | p (uncorr) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dlPFC | beta | +0.00716 | +0.02449 | +0.01733 | 0.008 |
| IFG/vlPFC | beta | +0.00808 | +0.02384 | +0.01576 | 0.009 |
| dmPFC/SMA | beta | +0.00661 | +0.02055 | +0.01394 | 0.023 |

`dlPFC beta` and `IFG/vlPFC beta` are in the top 8 of BOTH windows, and this is
the same prefrontal-beta cluster the domain model's Cognitive row nominated. One
observation, three ways of slicing it.

**The balance did NOT buy power, and that is worth recording.** Going from
17-vs-34 to 26-vs-25 left the median interaction SE at 0.00641, against 0.00643
before — unchanged to three significant figures. So the binding constraint on
this contrast is the TOTAL number of subjects and the between-subject variance,
not the allocation between arms. Re-labelling cannot fix it; only more subjects
can. That kills "rebalance the strata" as a way forward and is the strongest
argument for taking the prefrontal-beta cluster to the hold-out cohort instead.
