## 16:20 — Missingness scan to set the decoder's channel/epoch eligibility

**Ran:** Two scratch probes over the 45 discovery subject-sessions with >=30
epochs, to set `architecture.md` PART 7's Y and Z on structural grounds before
any decoder touches a pain score. Masks + epoch_defs only — no cache reads, so
this cost minutes rather than hours. Mask
`std10_rv-gross-std3_satmargin15_sw_logz4` (the one the chan-view and slope
arrays already use; P0.1 remains open and every number here is conditional on
it).

**Output:** none persisted — scratch probes under `$SCRATCH/probe/`, not
committed. Numbers recorded in DECISIONS 2026-09-08.

**Saw:**

- Missingness is small: cohort-mean **2.4%** of channel-epoch cells excluded at
  `max_excluded_frac=0.5`, median 1.7%, worst unit 9.3%.
- The epoch distribution is **bimodal with an empty gap**: per-epoch
  bad-channel fraction has median 0, p95 = 0.067, p99 = 0.689. Nothing lives
  between ~7% and ~69%. Z anywhere in [0.2, 0.7] drops the same ~1% of epochs,
  so the threshold does no work — the data separates itself. Z = 0.5 chosen as
  the middle of the gap, which happens to be the existing
  `EPOCH_MAX_EXCLUDED_FRAC`.
- The channel tail is smooth (p95 = 0.086, p99 = 0.283) so Y does real work.
  Y = 0.2 chosen: channels are cheap for a decoder (6 features of ~918), epochs
  are not (~2% of a 48-observation training set).
- Cascade at Y=0.2/Z=0.5 keeps **98.9% of epochs and 97.2% of channels**, and
  **no unit drops below 30 epochs** (min 33). Cohort survives QC intact.
- Residual cells after both rules are **0.82% of the matrix but scattered
  across 23.6% of epochs** — so dropping rows to purge them would cost a
  quarter of the training data to remove under 1% of cells. Forces imputation.
- **Correction to my own first pass:** the cascade probe counted "channel absent
  from a run's montage" as bad, inflating channel loss. Only **2 of 45 units
  have run-varying montages** — 189 (120 of 230 channels absent from some of its
  36 runs) and 167 (26 of 169) — and those are exactly the two with the largest
  apparent channel loss, for 167 identically so. Corrected artifact-driven loss
  is ~1.5%, matching the first scan. Coverage and artifact need separate
  policies; a never-recorded channel must never be imputed.
- Per-session eligibility (one model per session) costs sub-209 its ses-02:
  45 epochs in ses-01, **17 in ses-02**, below the >=30 floor.
- sub-067 is an outlier worth remembering: **6 bipolar pairs from a single
  right-insula depth electrode** (RI1-RI6 plus RI13-RI14; the RI6->RI13 gap
  means contacts were dropped upstream of the cache), 3 of 6 in white matter. At
  36 features vs 34 epochs it is the only unit where p < n.
